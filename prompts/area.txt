COMMAND: `area: [zipcode, city]`  
→ SECTION 3: Area & Neighborhood Information

- Use the provided ZIP code and city to retrieve factual local context.
- You may issue multiple `web_search(query=...)` calls to complete missing or detailed information.
- Use only **verifiable and recent sources**. Do **not** interpret or generalize any statistics, especially those related to race, age, crime, or income.
- Present the output in a clean bullet list grouped under the following headings:

---

`Demographics:`  
- Total Population  
- Population Density (per sq mi/km)  
- Age Distribution (e.g., under 18, 18–34, 35–64, 65+)  
- Racial Composition (percentages only)  
- Household Income Brackets (e.g., % earning under $50K, $50K–$100K, etc.)

---

`School Ratings:`  
- For each notable public school (within approx. 5 miles):  
  - Type (Elementary, Middle, High)  
  - Name  
  - Distance from ZIP code center  
  - Rating or grade (if available)

---

`Crime Rate:`  
- Crime Score or Index (numerical if available)  
- Relative Comparison: Higher / Average / Lower than state or national average  
- Source must be cited if provided in the result

---

`Walkability:`  
- Walk Score (0–100)  
- Optionally include Transit Score and Bike Score if available

---

`Points of Interest:`  
- Categorize by type:  
  - Grocery Stores / Markets  
  - Restaurants / Cafes  
  - Parks / Green Spaces  
  - Shopping Centers / Malls  
  - Hospitals / Clinics  
- Include 2–3 examples per category, if available

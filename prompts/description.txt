COMMAND: `description: [property address]`  
→ SECTION 1: General Property Introduction

- Use `web_search` to retrieve high-level details about the property at the specified address.
- Write a short **introductory paragraph (2–3 sentences max)** describing the type of home, layout, and appeal.
- Include:
  - Property type (e.g., single-family home, condo)
  - Bedroom and bathroom count
  - General layout (e.g., split floorplan, open-concept)
  - Design style or construction era (e.g., mid-century, contemporary, brick exterior)
- Mention nearby area attributes only if they are clearly available (e.g., neighborhood name, proximity to landmarks).
- Do **not** use vague or marketing language like “inviting” or “perfect” unless explicitly supported by the data.
- The output must be in paragraph form — **no bullet points**.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
"This beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom brick home at 2556 Burlingame Dr W, Jacksonville, FL, features a classic mid-century layout with refinished hardwood floors and an attached garage. Built in 1957, the 1,488 sq ft property offers an open floor plan ideal for entertaining, and is situated on a spacious 0.25-acre lot in the Arlingwood neighborhood."

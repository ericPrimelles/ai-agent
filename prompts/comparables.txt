## COMMAND: `comparable: [property address]`  

### Process  

- Search for **recently listed or sold homes within 0.5–1 mile radius** of the target property.  
- Ensure comparables are in the **same school district** and consider **rivers, highways, or train tracks** that may influence values.  
- Prefer homes sold in the **last 3–6 months** (12 months if the market is very stable).  
- Collect as many of the following attributes as possible for each comparable.  

### Comparable Fields  

- Address  
- Listing Status (Active / Pending / Sold)  
- Listing Price  
- Closing Price (if sold)  
- Living Area (sq ft)  
- $/Sq Ft  
- Bedrooms  
- Bathrooms  
- Lot Size (sq ft or acres) and Shape (if available)  
- Garage (size/type)  
- Year Built  
- Recent Renovations / Condition (e.g., remodeled, original, needs work)  
- Finishes (floors, kitchen, bathrooms, windows, roof, HVAC)  
- Style and Design (e.g., ranch, contemporary, colonial)  
- Type of Construction (single-family, townhome, condo [include floor], multifamily [2–4 units])  
- Days on Market  
- Seller Motivation (regular sale, foreclosure, short sale)  
- Listing URL  

---

---

### Summary Section  

After listing at least 3 comparables, include:  

- Comparable Price Range: lowest – highest  
- Average $/Sq Ft  
- Typical Property Profile: summarize beds, baths, sqft, year built range  
- Summary Comparison: 1–2 factual sentences on how the subject property compares statistically  

---

### Example Search Queries  

- `web_search(query="Recently sold homes within 1 mile of [property address]")`  
- `web_search(query="Homes sold last 6 months near [property address] same school district")`  
- `web_search(query="Price per square foot homes near [property address]")`  
- `web_search(query="Garage details for homes near [property address]")`  
- `web_search(query="Renovations or condition of [property address] comparable homes")`  
- `web_search(query="Days on market for homes near [property address]")`  
- `web_search(query="Foreclosure or short sale homes near [property address]")`  

---

⚠️ Important: Continue issuing `web_search(query=...)` until most comparable fields are populated but not more than 10 times. If data cannot be found, omit the field. Never speculate.
